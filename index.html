Why Nova

A star that flares, then settles

A nova is a star that suddenly brightens — more light in a single night than it gave in a century. It is not an explosion that destroys anything. It is a threshold being crossed.

Coffee has the same threshold. It sits between first crack and second, and the roaster who reaches it holds the bean there for less than a minute before dropping it. Push past it and you taste ash. Stop short and you taste grass.

The three curls of steam in our mark are not decoration. They are the last forty seconds of the roast.
